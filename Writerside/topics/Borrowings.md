# Borrowings

This section in the **Library Module** of LIPA ADA manages all activities related to issuing and returning books.
It helps track which student has borrowed which book, the borrowing date, return date, and status (e.g., returned or overdue).

## Navigation Steps
Step 1: Open Library Module
  - From the left sidebar, click on Library.
  - Select Borrowings from the sub-menu.

Step 2: View Existing Borrowings
  - You will see a table listing all currently issued books.
  - Each entry displays the book, student, and borrowing status.


![](issue books.PNG)

## How to Issue a Book
Step 1. Click **Add Issue Book**
   - In the top-right corner of the Borrowings page, click the **+ Issue Book** button.
   - A form titled **Add Issue Book to student** appears.

Step 2. Fill in Borrowing Details
  - Enter the required information **Book**, **Student** , **Start Date**, **Description**. 

Step 3. Save the Record
  - Click **Issue Book** to confirm the borrowing transaction.
  - The book will now appear under the Borrowings list with status: **Borrowed**.
