# Setup

Ensure you have registered all your **Classrooms** under **Student Records Module**

To begin using the admission module, follow these steps to complete the setup:


---

1. **Open Settings**![](adminsettingup.PNG)
2. **Enter Admission Fees**  
   Specify the admission fee for each classroom.![](admissionfee update2.PNG)
3. **Enable Admissions**  
   Open the admission process by selecting **Yes**.![](admission open2.PNG)
4. **Select Academic Years**  
   Choose the academic years for which admissions should be open.![](admission yearupdate2.PNG)
5. **Add a Notice**  
   Write any notice you want displayed at the top of the admission page.
6. **Set Admission Terms and Conditions**  
   Clearly outline the terms and conditions, such as whether admission is guaranteed or if the admission fee is refundable.![](admission noteupdate2.PNG)
7. Click **Save Chages** to finish
